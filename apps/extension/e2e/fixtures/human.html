My weekend fixing the fence

Saturday morning the dog got out again through the gap by the old maple, so I finally dragged the post-hole digger out of the shed. Took me three tries to get the first post straight because the ground out there is basically clay once you get past the first six inches, and I don't own a level that isn't slightly warped from the flood two summers back.

My neighbor Frank came over around eleven with a thermos of coffee and told me, again, about the time his fence blew down in '09. He held the string line while I set the second and third posts, and we argued for a while about whether to use two rails or three. Went with three in the end because the dog is a jumper.

By four the light was going and I still had one panel left, so that's tomorrow's problem. My back is going to hate me for this on Monday, but the gap is closed and the dog stayed in the yard all evening, so I'm calling it a win for now.

Total cost: about ninety bucks in lumber, a bag of concrete I already had, and one trip to the hardware store for a box of screws because I stripped half the ones I started with.
